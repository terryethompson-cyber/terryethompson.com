Which One To Send

Use SVG whenever the other side will take it. It stays sharp at any size, on a business card or a banner, and it is the one a printer wants. If their system rejects it, send the 1024 PNG. Use the white version on dark backgrounds and photos, and the black version for one-color printing, faxes and forms.

If They Need A PNG

Fixed sizes, when SVG will not do.

Some older systems will not take an SVG. Send the largest one that fits their limit: a PNG can be scaled down cleanly, never up.

1024 px · print and large format 512 px · web and social 180 px · phone icon 32 px · browser tab

The 1024 is the one to send a printer if they reject the SVG. The 512 covers profile pictures and email signatures. The two small ones are what your site already uses for the browser tab and the phone home screen, so you will rarely need to send them.
